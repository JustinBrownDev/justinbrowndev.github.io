POST-HANDOFF STREAMING POLICY
- Spawn chunk 0,0 remains the only startup gate.
- Normal movement/jump/F/P/pointer-lock runtime starts immediately after spawn READY.
- No 120ms one-chunk drip timer remains.
- One cooperative multi-chunk pump stays active after handoff.
- 5x5 READY render neighborhood target (radius 2).
- 7x7 warm prefetch neighborhood target (radius 3).
- retention radius 4 to avoid boundary churn while keeping memory local.
- Up to 4 chunks per pump while render ring is incomplete.
- Up to 3 chunks per pump while prefetch ring is incomplete.
- Queue refreshes between chunks so movement during a long pump reprioritizes immediately.
- Current camera heading biases equally-near chunks in front of the player.
- Chunk construction still yields on frame/input pressure; this is aggressive utilization, not a return to blocking whole-world loading.
- Streaming policy is centralized under CONFIG.streaming and therefore available to cfg.* reload parameterization.

LANDMARK POLICY
- Six world-singular authored areas remain confined to pinned spawn chunk 0,0.
- Infinite terrain gets repeatable/disposable district landmarks instead of more singulars.
- Exactly one district landmark is selected per 3x3 chunk macrocell.
- District landmark families: spire, stack, gatehouse, archive, beacon.
- Landmark identity/placement is coordinate deterministic and survives unload/regeneration.
- Landmarks reuse chunk instancing/batching instead of creating a large draw-call tax.

INFINITE WORLD CONTRACTS
- Stable/versioned chunk/entity/owner IDs retained.
- Weirdness gradient retained for future farther-from-spawn -> stranger content.
- Generic chunks build off-scene and commit render + physics atomically.
- Physics/render state is chunk-owned and removable.
- Far chunk scene/physics state and scheduler metadata are pruned.
- Pinned authored spawn remains resident during normal streaming.
- Shared road boundary portals remain deterministic across independently generated neighbors.
