JUSTINBROWNDEV.GITHUB.IO - PROGRESSIVE RUNTIME / LLM-STRUCTURE VALIDATION
Date: 2026-08-30

SCOPE
This release combines the conceptual code-separation pass with the measured progressive-runtime correction. The world content remains rich and deterministic; the changes reduce indivisible authored-generation work, keep structural rendering cheap while the world materializes, and preserve the existing atomic infinite-chunk contract.

STRUCTURE / LLM COMPREHENSION
- main.js reduced from the historical ~16.8k-line monolith to ~3.8k lines.
- Configuration, quantitative literals, adornment/network work, signature landmarks, ordinary buildings, facades, street props, ground surfaces, vertical circulation, spawn planning, signage, geometry helpers, material refinement, music, and themed corpora live in conceptual modules.
- Authored runtime prose comments are harvested into strict CODELORE strings; parser-based audit protects comment-like text inside strings/templates.
- Curated vocabulary is split into multiple semantic neighborhoods and consumed contextually rather than as one repeated global word bag.

CONFIRMED PROGRESSIVE-RUNTIME FIXES
- Rich signature landmarks are resumable generator/state-machine builders with semantic yield boundaries.
- Ordinary authored buildings yield at floors/interiors/roofs/facade-sign/detail boundaries.
- Individual facade sign placement is resumable below whole-facade granularity.
- The same player-physics runtime exists during authored construction and ingests late world collision incrementally.
- Static authored optimization begins incrementally during construction and performs local dirty-chunk flushes instead of waiting for a giant final pass.
- Structural bootstrap uses one inexpensive preview material, reduced pixel ratio, and no bloom while nearby world construction is latency-critical.
- Cosmetic panels/signs/fixtures may exist deterministically while hidden, then reveal later through a bounded nearest-first material/refinement controller.
- Full runtime begins before static authored refinement completes.
- Authored material refinement waits until the playable 5x5 streamed chunk ring is 25/25 READY.
- Material refinement reveals at most one previously-hidden cosmetic object per pump and uses a small time budget.
- Final pixel ratio/bloom are restored after refinement completes.
- Proactive shader-prewarm work is disabled in the shipping progressive path; construction does not wait on a mesh-by-mesh compile queue.
- Dynamic authored lights use a stable render-light pool rather than continuously changing shader light-count families.

INFINITE STREAMING
- WorldChunkStreamer remains the sole visibility/lifecycle authority for streamed infinite chunks.
- Generic chunks remain deterministic, direct-attached, atomically committed with owned physics, bounded-retention, and unloadable.
- Generic chunk construction remains intentionally atomic because measurement continues to show millisecond-scale cost.
- Latest Node throughput self-test for seed 671278205: 49/49 7x7 neighborhood READY, 25/25 render ring READY, ~70.5 ms total test CPU, ~1.38 ms average build, ~0.008 ms average commit/commit-to-visible.
